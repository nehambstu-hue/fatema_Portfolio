ABOUT

A data-oriented sustainability professional in Bangladesh's textile industry

I am an Environmental Science and Resource Management graduate working in the RMG and textile sector, with practical experience in environmental compliance, sustainability data management, chemical management, Higg FEM, ZDHC-related activities, resource monitoring and ESG-focused reporting.

My work sits at the intersection of environmental performance, sustainability data and responsible manufacturing. I've worked with factory-level environmental and sustainability information — energy, water, waste, chemicals, environmental compliance and social sustainability indicators — and coordinated the audits and documentation that make compliance claims verifiable.

I'm currently expanding my expertise in GHG accounting, Scope 1–3 emissions, decarbonization, GRI reporting, sustainability analytics, circular economy and emerging EU sustainability requirements such as ESPR and the Digital Product Passport (DPP). My long-term focus is contributing to measurable sustainability improvement across Bangladesh's RMG and textile value chain.
